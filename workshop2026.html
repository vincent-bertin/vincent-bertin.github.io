About the workshop

The workshop aims to bring together researchers from fluid mechanics, physics, and applied mathematics working on the interplay between fluid flows and deformable media, from fundamental questions to applied problems. The scientific program will be organized around three main themes:

  1. Fluid–Structure Interactions at low Re (elastohydrodynamics, lubrication, compliant boundaries)
  2. Soft Interfaces (wetting, capillarity, elastocapillarity, interfacial instabilities)
  3. Complex Fluid Flows (viscoelasticity, active matter, biofluidics)

Contributed talks are welcome. Please submit an abstract by 31 October 2026.

Registration is free, but mandatory.
